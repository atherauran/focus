# Sample credits

All samples are trimmed, normalized and re-encoded by `scripts/build-samples.mjs`.

| Instrument | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grand piano (Steinway B), upright piano (Yamaha), vibraphone, bowed vibraphone, glockenspiel, marimba, concert harp, drums & percussion | [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) | CC0 |
| String sections (violins, violas, celli), flute, clarinet | [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) | CC0 |
| Nylon-string guitar | [FreePats Spanish Classical Guitar](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html) | CC0 |
| Rhodes Mk I | [jRhodes3d by Jeff Learman](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d) | CC BY-NC 4.0 |
| Upright bass (pizzicato) | [D. Smolken double bass](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/dsmolken.double-bass) | CC0 |
